## Which pixel artists and small creators post "I turned your pet into pixel art", pet sprite commissions or game-style pet portraits on TikTok and Instagram?

### Takeaway
I found no article, listicle or Reddit thread that names a dedicated "I turned your pet into pixel art" TikTok creator. What does exist is a small set of commission sellers who promote on TikTok and Instagram (@artbypeji, Pixelesma, Asterbuns, sir_cat_is_cat), a pixel-pet-portrait shop (PixlPawPets / pixlpaw.com), and a TikTok Discover cluster called "Pet Pixel Studio" with a stated ~665.3K posts. Next to these sit a few "draw my followers' pets" and cozy-gaming creators, and the cozy-gaming creators are the closest benchmarks with stated follower counts under 100k.

## Which hashtags are used, and what do sources say about their size or trend status in 2025–2026?
