# Viral short-form pet video formats (TikTok and Instagram Reels), US and Europe, 2025 to October 2026, and their hook patterns

Method note for the report writer: web search worked, but fetching full pages was blocked by this environment's egress policy (knowyourmeme.com, goodgoodgood.co and meetglimpse.com all failed with "connect_rejected"/ENOTFOUND). Every finding below therefore comes from search-engine summaries of the cited pages, not from reading the full page. Numbers are reproduced only where the summary quoted them. Treat exact wording of hooks as "as summarised", not verified verbatim. Many pages that came up (accio.com, TikTok /discover/ pages, CapCut/Pippit template pages, SEO blogs) are auto-generated or vendor marketing; they are flagged where used.

## Q1. Which pet formats recur most in 2025–2026 (format catalog)

### Takeaway
The best-documented recurring pet formats of 2025–2026 are: (1) AI "turn my pet into X" (pet-to-human, Ghibli, AI dance, AI kung fu/soap-opera pets); (2) fake security/doorbell-cam animal clips (mostly AI-made); (3) "test your pet" reaction formats (wall/intelligence test, tell-your-cat-a-joke, fake-faint/fake-kidnap); (4) pet-personality POV comparisons (boy cat vs girl cat); (5) a sound or meme template that people refill with their own pet (Wi Wi Wi hopecore, Meowl/"New Soul", Cat Laughing reaction, "Today I'm gonna turn into a cute little X"); (6) before/after adoption glow-ups; (7) grooming/spa ASMR. Pet POV voiceovers and "day in the life" are everywhere but have no dated 2025–2026 trend coverage. I found no sourced 2025–2026 evidence that tier lists, "which one is your pet" personality tests or pet-cam "what my pet does while I'm away" were named trends.

### Inferences
- The strongest pattern in 2025–2026 is AI transformation of the pet, either into a still image (human, Ghibli) or a video (dance, kung fu, fake cam). Each wave followed a new model release: GPT-4o images in March 2025, Sora 2 in October 2025, Seedance 2.0 in 2026. Expect new waves with each tool launch.
- The second strongest is a reusable meme or sound template plus the viewer's own pet (Wi Wi Wi, Meowl, Cat Laughing, "cute little X", boy/girl cat). These have the shortest shelf life (weeks) and depend on catching the sound early.
- "Test" formats work because of a binary payoff and multi-pet comparison, but they attract welfare criticism. A brand should prefer benign versions (the joke test, the phone-in-face reaction) over restraint or scare tests.
- Real versus AI is now a theme in itself: the fake-cam clips spread through "is this real?" comments.

### Gaps
- No dated 2025–2026 sources found for tier lists or "ranking my pets", "which one is your pet" personality tests, "rate my pet's…", or genuine pet-cam "what my pet does while I'm away" as named trends.
- No sources found for Pixar, LEGO, plush, anime or pixel-art pet filter trends specifically, despite targeted searches.
- Typical video lengths are almost never stated. The only one is the 48-second @aguyandagolden wall-test clip.
- Creator handles for the bunny-trampoline clip and many AI clips are not stated in the summaries.

## Q2. Which hook structures and on-screen text patterns work best for pets

### Takeaway
Documented 2025–2026 hooks are short, templated lines tied to a trend or sound: "POV: you have a [boy/girl] cat", "POV: your dog's inner monologue when…", "Today I'm gonna turn into a cute little [X]", "[Pet] as a human", a "test" setup with a pass/fail reveal ("Dog intelligence test", "Teddy is the brains of the operation"), and before/after or "first day home vs now". I found no 2025–2026 source documenting "Tell me you have a cat without telling me" (a 2021-era meme format).

## Q4. Which formats are faceless and easy to recreate with phone footage of one's own pet plus on-screen text

### Takeaway
The most recreatable faceless formats documented for 2025–2026 are: AI transformation of a pet photo (pet-to-human, Ghibli or other style, posted as a before/after); sound-template pet edits (Wi Wi Wi hopecore, Meowl/"New Soul" slideshow, Cat Laughing green screen, "cute little X" cut-to-pet); boy-cat/girl-cat POV with text overlays; before/after adoption glow-ups; and pet POV inner-monologue text or text-to-speech over ordinary clips. Test formats (wall test, joke test) need the owner's voice or hands but not their face. AI micro-dramas and fake-cam clips need generative video tools, not one's own footage.

### Cited Findings
- AI pet-to-human and Ghibli need only one pet photo and a prompt — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/i-turned-dogs-into-humans-using-chatgpt-the-results-are-both-creepy-and-amazing-at-the-same-time); [Upsampler](https://upsampler.com/blog/iconic-images-in-studio-ghibli-style)

- Meowl trend posts are often TikTok Slideshows with sped-up "New Soul", and Wi Wi Wi edits are kitten clips plus nature montage plus "Dramamine". Neither needs a face — [KYM Owl Arm](https://amp.knowyourmeme.com/memes/owl-arm-trend); [KYM Wi Wi Wi](https://knowyourmeme.com/editorials/who-is-the-original-wi-wi-wi-cat-the-hopecore-tiktok-trend-explained)
