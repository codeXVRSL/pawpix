# PawPixel on Facebook: launch week

A one-week plan for a brand-new Page, built from what performs on Facebook in the Philippines right now, and from how indie pet games get their first thousand fans. Every graphic here is rendered from the app's own sprite engine, so what people see in the feed is exactly what they get on their phone.

Files: `profile.png` (page picture), `cover.png` (page cover), one graphic per day (`day1…day7`, the Day 2 carousel is three squares), and `poster.png` (stories, and print). Templates live in `../templates`; `render.sh` rebuilds everything.

## What the research says, in six lines

1. **Pets are the most engaged topic on Facebook in the Philippines.** Meta's own data has animal and pet content at the top of engagement for Filipino creators, ahead of comedy and sports. We are posting into the warmest category there is. ([GMA](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/lifestyle/hobbiesandactivities/856424/pet-related-reels-are-the-most-engaged-content-on-philippine-facebook-instagram-in-2022-me/story/), [BusinessWorld](https://www.bworldonline.com/technology/2023/01/13/498233/animal-and-pet-related-reels-get-most-engagement-on-facebook-and-instagram-in-2022/))
2. **Filipinos check Facebook at lunch and after dinner.** The two windows that hold every day of the week are 12–2 PM and 7–9 PM PHT. Main post in the evening, a second touch at noon when there is one. ([Spiralytics](https://www.spiralytics.com/blog/best-times-to-post-social-media-philippines/), [TeamAsia](https://www.teamasia.com/best-time-to-post-on-facebook-in-the-philippines-2026/))
3. **Short captions win.** Posts of 80 characters or fewer get about two thirds more engagement; 150–250 characters is the ceiling for a post that needs context and a call to action. Facebook folds the caption after about 477 characters on desktop, so the hook must be in line one. ([SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/ideal-social-media-caption-length), [Heropost](https://heropost.io/how-to-write-social-media-captions-2026-4/))
4. **Portrait images, 1080×1350.** Most people scroll on a phone; 4:5 takes the most screen. Carousels use 1080×1080. ([HeyOrca](https://www.heyorca.com/blog/facebook-posting-specs-best-practices-in-2026), [Recurpost](https://recurpost.com/schedule-facebook-posts/facebook-post-sizes/))
5. **83% of Filipino owners call their pet their baby.** The emotional register is "fur parent", not "user". Captions talk about the pet by name, never about features. ([Vero ASEAN](https://vero-asean.com/philippines-pet-culture-brand-opportunities/))
6. **Launch week for an indie game is: announce, show how it works, show the feeling, ask a question, show the daily use, invite content, then show the people behind it.** Pin the announcement, answer every comment the same day, and have the next month queued before you start. ([Wayline](https://www.wayline.io/blog/social-media-marketing-indie-game-developers-launch), [Altos](https://altosagency.com/blog/article/social-media-launch-checklist-facebook-edition), [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/facebook-marketing/facebook-post-ideas))

## Page setup (before Day 1)

- **Name:** PawPixel · **Category:** App page · **Username:** @pawpixelapp
- **Picture:** `profile.png` (the orange cat on coral, same as the app icon so the feed matches the phone).
- **Cover:** `cover.png`. The message sits in the middle 1200 px so the phone crop keeps it.
- **Bio (101 chars):** Your real pet, in pixels. Snap a photo, meet their twin, keep them fed. Free on Android. Naga City.
- **Action button:** "Use App" → Play Store link. Until the listing is live, "Send message".
- **Pinned post:** Day 1.
- **Reply voice:** first person plural, warm, Taglish, always use the pet's name from the comment. Reply within the hour during the two posting windows.

## The playbook around the posts

- **Groups, not ads, in week one.** Join 5 to 8 Philippine pet groups (Cat Lovers Philippines, Dog Lovers PH, Bicol pet groups, Naga buy-and-sell pet sections). Post the Day 1 image there only where rules allow, phrased as "we made this for our own cats, would love your feedback" and answer every reply. Group posts routinely out-reach a new Page's own posts.
- **Reels.** The single best format on Philippine Facebook is a short pet video. `docs/samples/live-pet.gif` is the seed: a 10–15 second screen recording of the room (pet wandering, a tap on a meter, the eating animation and hearts) with the Day 3 caption, posted as a Reel on Day 3 or Day 5 alongside the image. Keep it vertical, no music licence problems if you use Facebook's library.
- **Stories.** Post `poster.png` as a story on Day 1 and Day 5 with the link sticker.
- **Comments are the product.** Every comment gets a reply with the pet's name. A comment with a pet photo gets a reply offering to draw it ("send it to us in Messenger and we'll post the twin").
- **Measure three numbers only:** follows per post, comments per post, link taps (use a bit.ly for the store link). After week one, double down on the two formats that won.
- **Week two seed:** Monday feature post of the best #PawPixelTwin entries; a "name that mood" poll with the seven mood poses; a "what should we add next" vote (outfits, more coats, iOS); a Pet Studio reveal.

## Design notes (so the kit stays consistent)

- One message per graphic, headline in Pixelify Sans, everything else in Nunito. Text stays under 20% of the image.
- Cream paper, ink text, candy colours for care; white sticker panels with a sand lip, the same as the app's own buttons.
- The pet is always the biggest thing on the page, drawn by the real engine (`../assets`), never a stock illustration.
- The brand mark (coral square with the white paw and the wordmark) sits top-left on every feed graphic.
- Sizes: feed 1080×1350, carousel 1080×1080, cover 1640×624 (message in the middle 1200 px), story and poster 1080×1920, profile 1080×1080.
