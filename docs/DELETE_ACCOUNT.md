# Delete your PawPixel data

_Publish this page at a public address (for example `https://<your-site>/delete`) and give that
address to Google Play (Data safety → Data deletion) and in the App Store listing. Replace the
bracketed parts first._

## Most of PawPixel has no account
Your pets, photos, care tasks, health records and history live only on your phone. To delete them:
- In the app: **Settings → Delete all my data**, or
- uninstall PawPixel. (On Android, also remove PawPixel from your Google backup: Google Drive → Backups.)

## PawPixel account (pet map and household)
If you joined the optional pet map or a household, the server keeps your sign-in (Google or Apple account ID and email), your pixel pets, their names, your ~1 km area, your RSVPs, blocks and reports, and your household membership (the name you show) and the shared pets' care records.

Deleting the account removes your membership in a household (and the household itself if you were the last one in it). Care records you logged stay with the household, no longer linked to you.

**Delete it in the app:** Pet map → More → **Delete my map account** (or Settings → Delete all my data). It is deleted immediately.

**Can't open the app any more?** Email **[support email]** from the address you signed in with (or tell us the pet names you shared) with the subject "Delete my PawPixel map account". We delete the account and everything above within **7 days** and reply when it's done.

### What is kept
Reports you sent are deleted with your account. A report someone else sent about one of your pets
stays with the moderators, no longer linked to you, until it's closed.
